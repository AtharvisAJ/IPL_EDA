Read this carefully: at a 5% threshold, testing 14 venues independently predicts roughly 0.7 false positives by chance alone. A league-wide test across all venues at once (χ² = 11.1, df = 13, p = 0.60) found no significant venue effect overall. Treat the one highlighted bar as a lead worth re-checking in a few more seasons, not a proven venue trend.
